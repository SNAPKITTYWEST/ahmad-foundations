C_t = f_t \odot C_{t-1} + (1-f_t) \odot (v_t \otimes k_t).

\section{The Foundational Mechanics of Artificial Neurons}

\subsection{The McCulloch-Pitts Abstraction (1943)}

McCulloch and Pitts~\cite{mcculloch1943} abstracted biological neurons into
binary logic gates: discrete binary inputs $x_i \in \{0,1\}$, aggregation by
summation against a fixed threshold $\theta$, and a binary activation state:
\[
y = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \sum_i x_i \geq \theta \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
\]
The McCulloch-Pitts unit could compute logic---AND, OR, NOT circuits assemble
from it---but every parameter was fixed and hand-wired.  It could decide but
could not learn.  Part~II resurrects this exact unit, with its logic made
algebraically checkable, as the routing layer of a modern architecture.

\subsection{Rosenblatt and the Perceptron (1957)}

Rosenblatt~\cite{rosenblatt1958} introduced trainable weights $w_i$ and a
dynamic learning algorithm.  The unit computes a weighted sum and fires on
threshold:
\[
y = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \sum_{i=1}^n w_i x_i > \text{threshold} \\
                   0 & \text{otherwise,} \end{cases}
\]
defining a linear decision boundary $\sum_i w_i x_i + b = 0$.
The Perceptron learning rule is pure error correction:
\[
w_i \leftarrow w_i + \eta(y_{\text{true}} - y_{\text{pred}})x_i.
\]
The Mark~1 Perceptron (1958): 400 photocells, banks of potentiometers as
weights, electric motors turning the knobs during training.  Learning as
literal mechanical nudging.  Rosenblatt supplied a theorem: if the data are
linearly separable, the rule is guaranteed to converge.

\subsection{The Linear-Separability Bottleneck (1969)}

Minsky and Papert~\cite{minsky1969} proved that single-layer perceptrons cannot
compute XOR.  The funding collapse and first AI winter followed.  The lesson
was not ``perceptrons are dead'' but that depth is mandatory---which immediately
raises the credit assignment problem.

\section{Multi-Layer Perceptrons and the Credit Assignment Problem}

\subsection{The Problem}

A single-layer network knows which weight caused an error---every connection
runs directly to the output.  Add hidden layers and that transparency dies.
The \textit{credit assignment problem}: the error is observed at the output,
but it is mathematically unclear how much responsibility each hidden weight
holds.

\subsection{Backpropagation and Gradient Descent}

Backpropagation~\cite{rumelhart1986} solves credit assignment via the chain rule:
\[
\frac{\partial E}{\partial w_{ij}} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial y_j} \cdot
\frac{\partial y_j}{\partial z_j} \cdot \frac{\partial z_j}{\partial w_{ij}},
\qquad W \leftarrow W - \eta \nabla E(W).
\]
The overarching paradigm:
\[
\text{Initialize} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{Predict} \;\longrightarrow\;
\text{Loss} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{Nudge Weights} \;\longrightarrow\;
\text{Repeat.}
\]
The backward pass requires: (a) a gradient tape (every forward activation
stored until consumed by its derivative); (b) weight transport (the backward
pass reuses the transpose of forward weights, a symmetry no biological synapse
implements); (c) trace destruction.  Part~II keeps the loop and replaces the
audit.

\section{Evolution of Specialized Architectures}

\subsection{Convolutional Networks and AlexNet (2012)}

CNNs impose two spatial priors: local receptive fields and parameter sharing.
AlexNet~\cite{krizhevsky2012} scaled this with GPU acceleration; ReLU
activations $f(x) = \max(0,x)$; Dropout at $p=0.5$; data augmentation.
Roughly 61M parameters.  The 2012 ImageNet result that ended the second winter.

\subsection{Recurrent Networks, Vanishing Gradients, and the LSTM}

For sequences, RNNs introduced feedback loops---and inherited a multiplicative
curse: gradients shrink or explode geometrically with sequence length.  The
LSTM~\cite{hochreiter1997} repaired this with an additively updated cell state
guarded by multiplicative gates:
\[
c_t = f_t \odot c_{t-1} + i_t \odot \tilde{c}_t.
\]
Greff et al.~\cite{greff2017} showed that coupling the gates as $i_t = 1 - f_t$
(the CIFG variant) loses essentially nothing in performance while deleting a
quarter of the gate parameters and turning the cell update into a convex
combination.  The CIFG is the heart of Part~II.

\section{The Transformer Era}

\subsection{Self-Attention: Query, Key, Value}

The Transformer~\cite{vaswani2017} eliminates recurrence.  Every token vector
$X$ is projected into Query, Key, Value spaces:
\[
\mathrm{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \mathrm{Softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V.
\]

\subsection{Feature Superposition and Polysemantic Neurons}

When $N_{\text{concepts}} \gg d$, concepts are encoded as almost-orthogonal
direction vectors in $\mathbb{R}^d$.  Individual neurons become
\textit{polysemantic}, firing for multiple unrelated features.  Superposition is
why large models are capable and why they are opaque.  In the standard lineage,
superposition is an accident.  Part~II's induction layer makes it an engineered
mechanism.

\section{The Memory Cell: The LSTM, Lifted}
\label{sec:memory}

Take the LSTM cell.  Apply the CIFG coupling $i_t = 1-f_t$.  Lift: the cell
state stops being a vector $c_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and becomes a matrix
$C_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$, written by rank-1 outer products.

\begin{definition}[CIFG Matrix Memory Update]
Given previous memory $C_{t-1} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$, forget gate
$f_t = \sigma(W_f [h_t; x_t] + b_f) \in (0,1)^d$, value $v_t$ and key $k_t$:
\[
\boxed{C_t = f_t \odot C_{t-1} + (1-f_t) \odot (v_t \otimes k_t),
\qquad i_t \equiv 1-f_t.}
\]
\end{definition}

Three consequences: the update is $O(d^2)$ (rank-1 write, never a matrix
multiply); $C_t$ is a convex combination so the memory cannot blow up if
inputs are bounded; the gate is one dial, not two.

\subsection{The IMMA Expert Cell}

Five gates in one fused projection:
\begin{align*}
r &= W_r h_{\text{prev}}, \quad
g = \mathrm{LayerNorm}(W_g [x; h_{\text{prev}}; r]) \in \mathbb{R}^{5d}, \\
(f, o, v, k) &= \mathrm{chunk}(g, 5), \quad
f \leftarrow \sigma(f),\; o \leftarrow \sigma(o),\; i \leftarrow 1-f, \\
C &= f \odot C_{\text{prev}} + i \odot (v \otimes k), \quad
h = o \odot \mathrm{LayerNorm}\!\left(\mathrm{diag}(C) W_h^\top\right).
\end{align*}
The input-gate slot is computed and discarded---the CIFG constraint overwrites
it---kept in the projection so that ablating the coupling is a one-line change.

\subsection{Sum-Inversion: Storage and Retrieval Theory}

Let $C = \sum_{i=1}^n v_i v_i^\top$.  Query with $q_j = v_j$:
\[
C v_j = |v_j|^2 v_j + \sum_{i \neq j} \langle v_i, v_j \rangle v_i.
\]
Orthonormal storage makes cross-terms vanish: retrieval is exact.  The
realistic case:

\begin{theorem}[Approximate Retrieval under Incoherence]
\label{thm:retrieval}
If stored unit vectors satisfy $|\langle v_i, v_j \rangle| \leq \mu$ for
$i \neq j$ with $(n-1)\mu < 1$, then $\hat{v}_j = Cq_j/|Cq_j|$ satisfies
\[
|\hat{v}_j - v_j| \leq \frac{(n-1)\mu}{1-(n-1)\mu}.
\]
Under query noise $|\epsilon| \leq \delta$, an additional $\delta\,\sigma_{\max}(C)/|Cv_j|$
term applies---which is why the spectral bound on $C$ is not decoration: it
caps noise amplification.

Status: \SKETCH\ (stated in \texttt{lean4/SumInversionAgent.lean}); write-then-read
round trip is \VERIFIED\ numerically.
\end{theorem}

\section{MMEP: Credit Assignment Without the Tape}
\label{sec:mmep}

\subsection{Equilibrium Propagation, Inherited and Extended}

Equilibrium Propagation~\cite{scellier2017} trains an energy-based network in
two phases: relax freely to $h_{\text{free}}$; weakly clamp outputs toward the
target with strength $\beta$ to reach $h_{\text{nudged}}$; update from the
difference of local correlations.  As $\beta \to 0$ this recovers the true
gradient.  No error signal propagated backward; each synapse needs only its
own endpoints in two snapshots.

MMEP repairs EP's two weaknesses: convergence is forced by a spectral
constraint; memory is made explicit as the CIFG matrix state.

\subsection{Energy Function}

\begin{equation}
E(C, H, \alpha;\theta) =
\underbrace{\|y - f(x;\theta)\|^2}_{E_{\text{pred}}}
+ \underbrace{\lambda\max(0,\sigma_{\max}(W) - \lambda_{\max})}_{E_{\text{constraint}}}
+ \underbrace{-\sum_k \alpha_k \log \alpha_k}_{E_{\text{entropy}}}
+ \underbrace{\|\alpha\|_1}_{E_{\text{sparsity}}}.
\end{equation}

Every term is non-negative: $E \geq 0$ by construction.
Defaults: $\lambda_{\max} = 0.95$, entropy ceiling 0.20 nats.

\subsection{The Two Phases and the Whole Rule}

\textbf{Free phase} ($T_{\text{free}} = 20$): damped fixed-point iteration
\[
h_{t+1} = (1-\alpha_{\text{relax}})h_t
+ \alpha_{\text{relax}}\,\sigma\!\left(Wh_t + C_{\text{global}} + C_{\text{expert}}^{(k)}\right),
\]
until $|\dot{h}| < 10^{-5}$.  Inference is free-phase relaxation.
\textit{The answer is already in the fixed point.}

\textbf{Nudged phase} ($T_{\text{nudge}} = 4$): clamp only the output layer
toward the target with strength $\beta$ (default 0.1, annealed to 0.01).

\textbf{The entire learning rule:}
\[
\Delta W = \frac{1}{\beta}\!\left(h_{\text{nudged}} h_{\text{nudged}}^\top
- h_{\text{free}} h_{\text{free}}^\top\right), \qquad
\Delta b = \frac{1}{\beta}\!\left(h_{\text{nudged}} - h_{\text{free}}\right).
\]
No tape, no transport, no trace destruction.

\subsection{Convergence (Audit-Strengthened)}

\begin{theorem}[Lyapunov Convergence of the Free Phase]
\label{thm:convergence}
Under $\sigma_{\max}(W) \leq \lambda_{\max} < 1$ for all weight matrices,
with SmoothLeaky activation satisfying $\sup_x f'(x) < 1$ (Axiom A1),
$E$ is a Lyapunov function for the free phase: bounded below, strictly
decreasing along non-equilibrium trajectories ($\dot{V} = -|\partial E/\partial h|^2 \leq 0$).
LaSalle's invariance principle gives convergence to the equilibrium set;
contraction gives uniqueness per input (Banach).

The composite relaxation map Jacobian is bounded:
\[
\left\|(1-\alpha_{\text{relax}})I + \alpha_{\text{relax}}\,\mathrm{diag}(\sigma'(Wh+\cdot))\,W\right\|
\leq (1-\alpha_{\text{relax}}) + \alpha_{\text{relax}}\lambda_{\max} < 1.
\]

\section{The Constraint Subsystem}
\label{sec:constraints}

\subsection{$H(\alpha) \leq 0.20$ Nats, Always}

Every routing decision passes through:
\[
\mathrm{ConstrainedSoftmax}(z, H_{\max}) = \mathrm{softmax}(z/\tau^*),
\qquad
\tau^* = \max\{\tau : H(\mathrm{softmax}(z/\tau)) \leq H_{\max}\},
\]
computed by bisection on temperature.

\begin{theorem}[Entropy Monotone in Temperature]
\label{thm:entropy-mono}
$H(\mathrm{softmax}(z/\tau))$ is non-decreasing in $\tau$, strictly increasing
unless $\mathrm{Var}_p(z) = 0$.

\textit{Proof.} Write $\beta = 1/\tau$, $p_i(\beta) = e^{\beta z_i}/Z(\beta)$.
Then $H(\beta) = -\beta\langle z\rangle_p + \ln Z(\beta)$.  Differentiating:
\[
\frac{dH}{d\beta} = -\langle z\rangle_p - \beta\frac{d\langle z\rangle_p}{d\beta}
+ \langle z\rangle_p = -\beta\,\mathrm{Var}_p(z) \leq 0,
\]
using $d\langle z\rangle_p/d\beta = \mathrm{Var}_p(z) \geq 0$.
For $\beta > 0$ ($\tau > 0$): $dH/d\beta \leq 0$, so $dH/d\tau \geq 0$.

Status: \PROVED\ (elementary; audit Finding 2 upgrades informal assertion to theorem).
\end{theorem}

\textit{Why 0.20?} Uniform over 4 experts carries $\ln 4 \approx 1.386$ nats;
0.20 forces $\approx 96$--97\% of mass onto one expert.  Dropout-strength
regularization expressed as an information-theoretic bound.

Fix a binary code matrix $B \in \{0,1\}^{d \times V}$ of full column rank
($d \geq V$).  Represent a sequence by trajectory $S_t = \sum_{i \leq t} B x_i$.
Predict the next increment $\Delta S_t$; decode:
\[
x_{t+1} = \arg\max\!\left(B^\dagger \Delta S_t\right).
\]
Full column rank makes noiseless reconstruction exact (\SKETCH; rank checked at
runtime).  Autoregression survives; the probability distribution over next
tokens does not.

\subsection{Adversarial Input Recovery: Formal Mechanisms}

Three principles govern the verification gauntlet's response to adversarial inputs:

\textbf{Principle 1 --- Tokenization and sequence entropy.}
An adversarially obfuscated prompt $p'$ satisfies
$H(T(p')) > H(T(p))$ and $|T(p')| > |T(p)|$ in expectation.
Layer~3 (Oracle) addresses this at the invariant-checking level:
the Z3/SPARK/Lean backends operate on token-independent logical structure.
Status: \VERIFIED.

\textbf{Principle 2 --- Attention dispersion and KV-cache degradation.}
A high-entropy prefix of length $n_a \ll N$ can dominate the attention mass
$A_l = \mathrm{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k})$.
Layer~4 (Interference) applies phase-mask cancellation; Layer~5 (Collapse)
gates on valid count, returning input unchanged if nothing validates.
Status: DERIVATION-SUPPORTED (\PROVED\ via
\texttt{destructive\_cancellation} and \texttt{constructive\_preservation}).

\textbf{Principle 3 --- State tensor realignment.}
The MMEP free-phase iteration washes out adversarially induced drift: any
perturbation orthogonal to the stable manifold decays at rate $\lambda_{\max}^t$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:convergence}).  The free phase performs context-level garbage
collection as a theorem.
Status: \PLAUSIBLE.

\subsection{Adversarial Audit Responses}
\label{sec:audit}

Six findings from the August 2026 adversarial audit: multi-agent debate
(Proponent, Adversary, Independent Verifier, Implementation Auditor, Red Team)
and first-principles re-derivation.

\subsubsection*{Finding 1 --- Trace Conservation: \REFUTED\ as Stated, Corrected}

Original claim: $\forall t: \mathrm{Tr}(C_t) = \mathrm{Tr}(C_0)$, reported \VERIFIED.

Audit verdict: \REFUTED.  Counterexample: $d=2$, $C_0=0$,
$f_t=(0.5,0.5)$, $v_t=k_t=(1,1)$ gives $\mathrm{Tr}(C_1)=1 \neq 0$.
No file in \texttt{tests/} computed $\mathrm{Tr}(C_t)$.

Corrected claim: trace is conserved iff
$\sum_i (1-f_t[i])(v_t[i]k_t[i] - C_{t-1}[i,i]) = 0$
(codimension-1; approached but not enforced exactly).
DEFUNCT status attaches to the theorem, not the cell.

Patch status: two required unit tests (consolidation regime: holds;
generic regime: fails) are specified and absent.
Severity: \textbf{CRITICAL}.

\subsubsection*{Finding 2 --- Constrained Softmax Entropy Monotonicity: \PROVED}

Audit verdict: THEOREM (elementary).  Proof given in
Theorem~\ref{thm:entropy-mono}.
\texttt{temperature\_bisection\_converges} is \PROVED\ in Lean.

\subsubsection*{Finding 3 --- CIFG Entrywise Boundedness: \PROVED}

Per entry $(i,j)$: $C_t[i,j] = f_t[i]\,C_{t-1}[i,j] + (1-f_t[i])\,v_t[i]k_t[j]$
is a strict convex combination (sigmoid never attains 0 or 1 for finite input),
hence bounded by induction.  Red Team counterexample (unbounded repeated writes)
refuted.

Patch status: \PROVED\ in \texttt{lean4/BURT\_IMMA\_Formalization.lean}.
Note: bounded $\neq$ trace-conserving (Finding 1 shows these are independent
properties; the original text's rhetorical proximity invited conflation).

\subsubsection*{Finding 4 --- Lyapunov Contraction: Gap Closed}

Composite-Jacobian bound derived explicitly (Section~\ref{sec:mmep}).
Eight load-bearing sub-theorems \PROVED\ in Lean.
Full Banach/LaSalle assembly: \SKETCH\ (one remaining obligation).
Severity: MAJOR (was already labeled \SKETCH; finding narrows which step remains).

\subsubsection*{Finding 5 --- BURT/IMMA Determinism: \DISPUTED}

BitmapIndex.search depends on previously indexed corpus---an external mutable
dependency outside the stated input triple.
IMMA is hybrid: Boolean-reducible in control flow, not in arithmetic core.
Severity: MINOR.  Determinism test across two floating-point backends: required,
not yet written.

\section{Limitations}

The convergence theorem is a sketch.  EP pays wall-clock tax:
$(T_{\text{free}} + T_{\text{nudge}}) \approx 24\times$ a backprop step at defaults.
The exact-decoding regime requires $d \geq V$; realistic deployment is
error-bounded, not exact.  All numbers here live at $d \leq 4096$ on one
consumer GPU.  Internal benchmarks are internal.  The trace-conservation claim
was wrong: the audit found it; this paper corrects it; the correction is not
fully verified.  The ablation suite has not run.

\section{Conclusion and Core Insight}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Part~I ends where the field stands: initialize, predict, calculate loss, nudge
weights, repeat.  Part~II keeps every word of that except the mechanism of the
nudge.  Learning should settle, not propagate.

The CIFG cell gives the system a memory whose writes are convex combinations
and whose reads carry error bounds.  Equilibrium propagation gives it a
learning rule a synapse could implement---two snapshots, one difference, no
tape.  The constrained softmax and spectral projection give it invariants that
hold by construction and are watched at runtime.  The SmoothLeaky activation
gives the fixed-point theorem its smoothness.  The Boolean actors give
Rosenblatt's unit the algebra Minsky said it lacked.  The superposition layer
takes the field's strangest emergent accident and puts it on the payroll.

The adversarial audit gave the paper something equally valuable: a refuted claim
(trace conservation), a proved theorem (entropy monotonicity), a derived bound
(composite Jacobian), and a clearly labeled disputed gap.  The ledger now has
approximately 70 proved statements, $\approx$20 sketches, one REFUTED claim
corrected, one DEFUNCT test specified and awaiting implementation.

\textit{That is the honest distance between what has been built and what has
been proved.}

The falsification suite is armed, the criteria are numbered, the fixed point is
waiting.
